The Residence

A Secretary's House, Reconsidered

Lekam Gedara takes its name from the Sinhala term for a secretary's residence — the heritage home once kept by the district's chief administrative officer. Built in the timeless vernacular style of colonial Ceylon, its hipped terracotta tile roof, deep eaves, and open verandahs have been meticulously restored.

Inside, the grand proportions remain untouched: high timber-beamed ceilings, wide doorways, and a generous verandah extending along the villa, framed by teak-shuttered windows painted in signature forest green. Modern luxury has been integrated with a gentle hand — whisper-quiet air conditioning, stone en-suite bathrooms, and ambient lighting.

Nestled on private coconut land in Thibiriyawa, the villa is reserved exclusively for one party at a time: your own private residence in Sri Lanka.
